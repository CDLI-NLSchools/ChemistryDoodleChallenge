# Chemistry Doodle Challenge

A single-file doodle challenge for Science 1206, Unit 2: Chemical Reactions, Section 1: Everyday Chemistry, Lesson 1 (Bonus Activity). It replaces a Jamboard activity that no longer works.

Note on the prompts: no copy of this activity's original board survives anywhere (not in the lesson, its videos, or the course files), so the eight doodle prompts were authored fresh from the lesson itself. The lesson is Everyday Chemistry, its videos cover matter, chemical reactions, cosmetics, batteries, food, fuels, cleaning products, pharmaceuticals, and plastics, and its learning goal is that students provide examples of how science and technology are an integral part of their lives and their community. Each prompt is a quick everyday chemistry doodle drawn from those topics: a battery doing its job, a chemical change in a kitchen, a cleaning product at work, a fuel in action, a medicine helping someone, a plastic object and where it ends up, matter changing state, and chemistry at work in the student's community.

Students pick any cards they like, draw in the built-in drawing space, and add a one-line caption ("What my doodle shows"). A live counter shows how many doodles are drawn. There is deliberately no finishing line: it is a bonus activity. At the end, the app compiles the completed doodles and captions into one printable doodle sheet that students can print or copy into their portfolio.

Formative only: nothing is scored, nothing is submitted anywhere, and nothing leaves the student's browser. Single HTML file, no external resources, works offline.
