Chemistry Doodle Challenge

Science 1206, Unit 2: Chemical Reactions, Section 1: Everyday Chemistry, Lesson 1 (Bonus Activity)

Here is a bonus activity if you are looking for some extra practice. Chemistry is at work all around us, every day: in batteries, kitchens, cleaning cupboards, medicines, and more. The learning goal for this lesson is to provide examples of how science and technology are an integral part of our lives and our community, and every doodle below is one of those examples.

Pick any doodle card, draw your idea in the drawing space, and add a caption that explains what your doodle shows. Do as many cards as you like: there is no finishing line here. Your doodles stay on this page: nothing is sent anywhere. When you are done, print or copy your doodle sheet to keep it with your work.
